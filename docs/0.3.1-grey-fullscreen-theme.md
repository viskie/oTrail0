# 0.3.1 — grey fullscreen theme

- Shared grey background/surfaces, dark readable text, condensed body typography and medium-weight button labels. Primary accents are #0078FF, #BD00FF, #FF9A00, #01FF1F and #E3FF00 from https://www.color-hex.com/color-palette/33554. A darker blue text variant preserves contrast for small labels.
- Map, navigation and routing/download buttons use compact rounded controls with consistent outlines, disabled states and a yellow ripple. Navigate is filled blue, Settings is purple. Native dialogs inherit the app typography and accents.
- Navigation uses coral #FF7F50. The first five trail colors are #17DEEE, #FF7F50, #FF4162, #ECF284 and #10AEB2 from https://www.color-hex.com/color-palette/5338. The remaining 26 are drawn from https://siter.io/retro-color-palette. All 31 pass colors are unique. Light trail colors get a dark halo for visibility over basemaps.
- Preserves the user's manually chosen continuity color #00FBFF as a separate overlay; does not change pass-detection or trail geometry. Palette settings now show swatches with readable hex labels.
- Portrait and landscape hide Android system bars using immersive mode; edge swipes reveal transient system controls. App title/strapline rows are hidden. IME insets are respected when entering text.
- More map space: reduced row spacing, hidden redundant tip/legend rows, compact navigation text and controls, a single-line road strip, and a narrower landscape sidebar. All prior actions, history, Stops, road lookup, navigation and settings remain available. Map attribution remains visible.
- No journal schema or calculation changes. A pre-update archive of databases/preferences was saved on the connected phone as files/pre-theme-backup.tar.
