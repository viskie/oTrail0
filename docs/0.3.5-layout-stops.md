# 0.3.5 — layout, map labels and stop distances

The destination is centered at 18sp with two lines; turn instructions remain 14sp. Total stats are pinned below the landscape sidebar's scrolling controls. Settings buttons occupy three columns in landscape. Buttons have larger italic lettering, reduced inner padding and visible gaps. Inner sidebar cards have horizontal insets. Editable spectrum gradients retain the manually configured base colors.

Offline city labels scale through four zoom bands and big cities are red italic. Long stops (default at least 60 minutes) have red italic labels; when their projected label bounds intersect cached offline city/town bounds, they become bold italic and shift above the city label. This collision check approximates the map renderer's label placement. Annotations are limited to 16 visible long stops at zoom 8 or above. The existing 31 trail colors are unchanged.

Recorded-stop rows and details include kilometers and average speed since the previous stop, computed from the collected journal. Existing data is read without changing the journal schema. Map data stays on the phone; city names come from the installed offline map.

Validation: assembleDebug, 53 JVM tests and lintDebug passed. Installed in place on POCO; destination/sidebar, settings grid and recorded-stop distances/red long-stop rows inspected. Database integrity check returned ok; all 69,513 raw fixes and 76 stop IDs retained, with zero missing old raw fixes or stop IDs. No new phone instrumentation suite was run for this UI update.
