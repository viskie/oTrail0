# 0.4.11 — black recorded-trail outline

All recorded trail strokes use a solid black 6dp casing around their existing 4dp inner color, including repeated-pass colors and inferred continuity links. The ongoing ride retains its existing neon-pink inner color and turquoise casing. Navigation styling is unchanged. History mode outlines every recorded stroke in black, even when its palette color matches live neon pink.

TrailPalette.Stroke carries an explicit ongoing flag set only by the live-run highlighter; outline choice does not rely on color equality. Existing three-argument construction defaults to recorded. UiConfig.TRAIL_OUTLINE and LIVE_TRAIL_OUTLINE configure the two casings separately. No changes to recorded coordinates, stop times, storage or GPS collection.
