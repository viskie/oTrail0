# 0.3.2 — GPS uncertainty and configurable native text

Confirmed stops survive poor accuracy, impossible speed reports at an unchanged position, a single distant fix and a lost signal. Departure requires coherent movement: at least 20 seconds with four precise moving fixes and at least 60 metres of progress, or 90 seconds and 200 metres of coherent progress under reduced accuracy. The original departure observation is retained once movement is confirmed. Genuine movement is tested separately from synthetic GPS drift.

At tracker restart, recent saved fixes are replayed through the detector. Previously split stops can recover their earlier arrival when three stable co-located observations span at least ten seconds. No coordinate rows are removed or changed, and saved stop IDs/labels are retained. Repairs write the previous timestamps and recovery reason to sync_logs. An overlapping open stop at the same place is closed at its last observed time with a SUPERSEDED audit entry; its ID and saved labels remain intact. A short uncertain excursion during an ongoing stop also recovers directly on stable reception. Stops remain provisional through uncertainty; consistent false GPS movement can still resemble genuine travel, so this is conservative filtering rather than a guarantee of physical position.

The live status keeps the retained stop duration visible, shows a weak-GPS note and suppresses unreliable speed. The location dot stays at the stop anchor during uncertainty, and navigation updates wait for trustworthy fixes. The status can use two lines rather than truncating the signal explanation. History and normalized raw fixes remain available.

Native selectors, switches, inputs, dialogs, lists, map popups and attribution now use INK; text helpers honor their explicitly supplied colors. Buttons keep BUTTON_TEXT and BUTTON. Main text accents use ACCENT_TEXT; muted text uses MUTED. Offline city/town labels use CITY_LABEL_*; other map lettering uses MAP_ROAD_LABEL_COLOR, MAP_OTHER_LABEL_COLOR, MAP_TEXT_OUTLINE_COLOR and MAP_TEXT_FONT. The latter two label colors default to INK. Online raster labels are controlled by their providers. Existing manual font/color choices are preserved.

Verification: 52 JVM checks and lint pass; 24 isolated phone checks cover backup/migration/data integrity, shared database concurrency, restart recovery, poor GPS, retained raw fixes and native text styling. Test fixtures use the validation package, separate from the production journal.
