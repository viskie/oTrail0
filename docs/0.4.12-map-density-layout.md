# 0.4.12 — zoom-aware stop dots and floating map totals

StopDotOverlay runs before saved-stop markers, adapting their visibility and diameter directly to camera zoom/pan/rotation without rebuilding the trail or waiting for GPS. StopDotLayout uses screen-space spacing from 46dp at zoom 7 to 18dp at zoom 16, normal diameters from 6dp to 14dp, with larger long-stop dots. Long stops take priority, then newest stops; a spatial grid suppresses nearby redundant dots. The live GPS marker is always retained with clear space around it. Hidden stop markers are disabled for both drawing and hit testing, while all stops remain in the database and Stops list. Zooming closer restores finer detail. All trail lines, black outlines, live pink run and navigation remain intact.

Totals move from the right sidebar to a one-line horizontally scrollable floating strip at the bottom of the map, reserving space for attribution at the left and a small bottom gap. Landscape top controls remain above a vertically centered Bijnor/navigation and nearby-information group; Resonations is pinned to the sidebar bottom. The center group scrolls on shorter displays.

Neon border strokes are inset by their full 6dp outer half-width so the outside follows rounded geometry without cutting off the glow. No outer clipping mask is used. Panel content gets clearance from the inner bright rim; buttons retain their existing geometry and palettes.

No changes to GPS collection, recorded coordinates, stops, database schema, playlists, modules, maps or downloads.
