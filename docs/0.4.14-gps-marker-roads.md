# 0.4.14 — selectable GPS artwork and quieter offline roads

Settings → GPS marker style offers 1 · Robot snake (default) and 2 · Classic dot. GpsMarkerStyle.OPTIONS is a registry with stable string IDs, labels, drawable resources and sizes; add future options there without renumbering persisted choices. The selected ID is stored in separate gps_marker preferences and survives ordinary upgrades. The original circle retains its status colors and accurate-GPS smile. The art marker retains a status-colored ring, upright centered anchoring, and a larger popup clearance; stop decluttering reserves enough room for its size. Recorded history and stop dots remain unchanged.

The new artwork is saved as app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/gps_robot_snake.png. It uses the user's later PNG as the edit target; source files in Downloads/Pictures were not modified. Built-in imagegen produced a transparent cutout (alpha range 0..255), decoded with sampling and cached by drawable resource to avoid per-fix full-size image allocation. UiConfig.DEFAULT_GPS_MARKER_STYLE and GPS_ART_MARKER_SIZE_DP configure its default and size.

Imagegen prompt used: “Edit target: this exact uploaded robotic snake illustration. Make a faithful transparent-background cutout by removing ONLY the entire baked grey checkerboard backdrop and the bottom-right white sparkle watermark. Preserve the snake's shapes, face/eye, colors, mouth, wires and colorful wire accents. Do not redesign, add anything, enlarge the eye, or paint an opaque background. Every background pixel must have alpha zero; only the original snake subject and its wire accents should remain, with clean anti-aliased edges. Tight transparent margins, centered full subject. This output is a small Android GPS marker asset, not a new illustration.”

The open India offline basemap uses dark navy motorways, near-black trunk roads, muted dark-violet primary roads, dark-brown secondary roads, grey tertiary/local roads and brown paths, with near-black casings. Only highway line strokes are restyled; widths, zoom rules, dash patterns, water, forest and navigation/trail overlays are preserved. Road names use dark navy. City/settlement names are brown with a light outline, including major cities. Theme cache keys include the road palette and city styling so stale colored tiles do not hide the update. This affects the locally rendered India offline map; provider-owned OSM raster and Esri imagery are not recolored.

All road and city colors are named UiConfig tokens. No GPS journal or stop migrations, and no map-data download is required for this styling change.
