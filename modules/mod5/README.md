# Reports — daily, weekly, monthly and custom trail reports

Interactive reports computed from recorded GPS fixes, stops and drive legs. Pick a period or a custom date range, see distance and moving-time cards, tap a chart bar to drill into that interval, and read derived findings such as the busiest interval, longest drive leg, most active hour, farthest point, longest stop and most visited place. Requires host 0.4.30 (53) and Android 10+.

1.0.1 — Daily, weekly and monthly views open on the period holding the newest recorded fix, so a period with no GPS data never looks like an all-zero report. Hour and day bars are built with calendar steps (correct across month and daylight-saving edges), a drive crossing midnight or an hour boundary is split between the two bars instead of being dropped, every interval with distance keeps a readable label even below 1 km, and the chart scrolls to the most recent interval with data.
